Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. We'd love to welcome you to Smyl Dental. Book online above or call 1300 007 695 and our team will find a time that suits you, usually within the same week.

How does the no gap check up and clean work?

If you have private health insurance with dental extras, we claim through HICAPS on the day and your fund covers the check up and clean, so there's no out of pocket cost in most cases. Eligible children are bulk billed under the CDBS. If you don't have cover, it's a flat $199.

Do you offer emergency dental appointments?

Yes. If you have a toothache, dental trauma or a lost crown or filling, call us as soon as you can. We're open all 7 days and do our best to see emergencies the same day.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. Let us know when you book. We'll never rush you, we'll explain everything first, and happy gas or IV sedation is available for suitable patients.

Where are you located and is there parking?

We're at Unit 3, 20 Success Circuit, Augustine Heights QLD 4300, with free onsite parking right outside. We see patients from across Springfield, Springfield Lakes, Springfield Central, Brookwater, Spring Mountain, Bellbird Park, Redbank Plains and the wider Ipswich area.
